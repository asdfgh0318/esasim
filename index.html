ESASIM

Dogfight in small foam WWII fighters, cutting each other's 10 m paper streamers, by the rules of ESA (Electric Simple Aircombat).

This game is bait. It exists to get you flying ESA air combat for real. If you like it here, the real thing is a foam fighter of 70 to 86 cm span and at most 450 g, a 10 m crepe-paper streamer on the tail and up to seven pilots in the sky at once.

Fly it for real

  • Rules: ESA regulations, still valid in 2026 (2026 calendar)
  • Planes: ESA kits at ef3m.pl, EPP kits at napolskimniebie.pl
  • How to start: build guide, ESA for beginners
  • Teams and contests: ESA squadrons and announcements, Eskadra Kraków
  • Watch: contest, Dobroszyce 2016
  • Abroad: EPA class in Germany/Austria (a sibling class), E1000 streamer combat (USA), Czech ACES association, ACES national sites
