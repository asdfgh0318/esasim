## Worldwide and related classes (second pass, same day)

## Plans and 3D models

- The ACES forum has many **3D-printed models** (Fiat G.55, Hellcat, Mustang, Tempest, Ki-61 and more) shared by one builder, with a thread of announcements (https://www.aircombat.pl/ACES/forum/viewtopic.php?t=5, updated 27.01.2026). **These are ACES-class (1:12, up to about 1.5 kg; the thread mentions a 1.7 kg multi-engine limit), not ESA.** The files sit behind forum threads; I did not download any.
- ESA planes are flat-fuselage EPP kits (shops above). For the sim, the geometry is simple enough to model by hand or procedurally if no glTF arrives (flat fuselage, flat tailplane, wing with an airfoil).
- Free-plan collections that turned up in search (not read, not verified): pfmrc.eu "darmowe plany", mds-rc.xt.pl, plans.rcmodell.hu, rc-rzeszow.pl downloads. Check licences before using any.

## Things the sources say that matter for the sim

- A first model recipe (guide at ef3m): any 3-channel radio, brushless motor with a prop saver, ESC, 2 micro servos (ailerons and elevator), LiPo ≤ 15 Wh. ESA has a Polish forum "Giełda" (classifieds).
- The shop guide says max single-engine mass **400 g**; the 2024 regulation (§3.6.2) says **450 g**. The regulation governs. Another shop says 600 g (that is the multi-engine limit).
- The ESA Polish scene organises: yearly national cup, national championship (2026: Brzeziny), squadrons, "Core" results database at aircombat.eu.
