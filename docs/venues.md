# Real venues used as visual reference (2026-10-04)

The scenery follows how real model-flying contests look: plain mown grass, white or red-white tape, a tree line, banners and tents. Sources are kept locally in `papers/venues/` (third-party pages, gitignored because they contain contact details); photos are used only as reference and are not in the repo.

| Venue | What I read | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Łososina Dolna airfield (EPNL, Aeroklub Podhalański) | Wikipedia (PL/EN) and local pages: grass runway about 800 x 150 m, elevation about 253.9 m, hangars and a tower, model competitions held there in 1979 and 1983 | Mown grass, no packed-earth strip |
| Dobroszyce 2016 ESA contest (video frame) | Frame of the video listed in `docs/fly-for-real.md`: a green pitch, bare trees on the horizon, white tape on the ground | Grass, tape lines, tree line |

Not verified: German and Czech aerobatic contest scenery (web search found no usable photos), so banners, tents, flags, powerlines and wheat fields are generic and marked VISUAL in `client/field.js`. Banners carry made-up names, no real brands.
